This policy applies to personal data we process as a data controller when you access or use our Services, interact with us, or otherwise engage with our products.

a) Data you provide directly

b) Data collected automatically

  • Device and browser information

c) Data from third parties

  • Authentication providers (if you use social login)

Where required, we process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide and maintain the Services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to improve, secure, and operate our Services responsibly.
  • Consent: where required by law, including certain cookies/marketing uses.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable legal or regulatory duties.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics, and (where permitted) marketing.

We may transfer personal data to countries outside your jurisdiction, including outside the EEA/UK/Switzerland. When we do, we use recognized transfer safeguards as required by law, such as:

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Retention periods depend on data type, legal requirements, and operational needs. When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymize it.
